Screen motion

A prototype of how kudde moves between the screens inside an agent. The screens and text are stand-ins. What you're judging is the movement.

Spring feel
Speed
Animations
  1. Terminal and chat

    Tap the chat icon in the title bar, then the terminal icon. The old view fades out, and the new one fades in while growing slightly into place. No slide, because a sideways swipe already means "next agent".

  2. Open Changes

    Tap the ± icon. Changes slides in from the right, and the terminal shifts a little to the left and dims behind it.

  3. Swipe back from Changes

    Drag sideways anywhere on Changes and hold. The screen follows your thumb, shrinks to a card and shows the terminal behind it. Let go past a third of the way (or flick) to go back. Drag back toward where you started and it cancels. On the phone this is Android's own back swipe from the edge.

  4. Tab overview

    Tap the square with the 3. The terminal shrinks into its own tile, and the other tabs come in after it. Tap another tile and that one grows to full screen. The back arrow returns to the tab you came from.

  5. Title bar menu

    Tap the three dots. The menu grows out of the button. A tap anywhere else only closes it; nothing underneath reacts.

  6. Spring feel

    Expressive is the feel of Android 16 on your Pixel: a little life in things smaller than the screen (the menu, the tiles). Standard is calmer. Whole screens never overshoot in either: you'd see a gap at the edge.

While anything moves, the screen ignores touches, so a tap mid-transition can't land on the screen underneath. That fixes the bug where a swipe reached the terminal behind the overview. With animations off, every change is an instant cut. Spring values follow Compose's Material 3 motion schemes; the app uses the same values without adding that library.
